Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Campus Seva's abroad counselling really free? +
Yes, completely free! Campus Seva charges zero consultancy fees from students. We earn from our partner universities — not from you. No hidden charges, no registration fees.
Which countries can I study in through Campus Seva? +
We help Indian students study in 7 countries: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Dubai (UAE), and New Zealand. Each destination has unique advantages — we help you pick the best fit for your goals and budget.
What are the eligibility requirements to study abroad? +
Requirements vary by country and university. Generally, you need: 10+2 with 55–70% marks for UG programs; Bachelor's degree for PG programs; English proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE); and sufficient financial proof. Our counsellors will evaluate your specific profile for free.
Can I work while studying abroad? +
Yes! Most countries allow part-time work during studies: USA (20 hrs/week on campus), UK (20 hrs/week), Canada (20 hrs/week), Australia (48 hrs/fortnight), Germany (120 days/year), UAE (with NOC), NZ (20 hrs/week).
How long does the entire process take from application to visa? +
On average 3–6 months for the full process. Some fast-track options exist. We recommend starting at least 6 months before your target intake. Germany can take longer (up to 9 months) due to language requirements.
Are scholarships available for Indian students? +
Absolutely! Many universities offer merit-based scholarships ranging from 10% to 100% of tuition. Government scholarships like Commonwealth, DAAD, Chevening, and Endeavour are also available. Our team proactively identifies scholarships you qualify for.
Which is the cheapest country to study abroad for Indian students? +
Germany tops the list — public universities charge almost zero tuition (only €250–€350/semester admin fee). New Zealand and Canada are affordable with good ROI. Germany requires German language proficiency for most programs though. Our counsellors will help you compare true cost of study including living expenses.
