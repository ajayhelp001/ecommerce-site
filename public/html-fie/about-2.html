Who We Are

One Team, From Coaching Selection to College Admission

Campus Seva was registered on 4th August 2025 to solve a problem every JEE and NEET aspirant's family faces — too many decisions, too little reliable guidance, and admission paperwork that's confusing even for parents who've done it before.

We provide guidance across coaching institutes, schools, hostels and colleges — and run a dedicated counselling desk for students after JEE and NEET results, handling JoSAA and private counselling rounds personally from our offices in Kota and Sikar.

✓

JoSAA & private counselling — every round handled by our IIT/NEET-qualified team

✓

Complete documentation support — admission paperwork managed end-to-end by Campus Seva

✓

In-person or online — visit our Kota/Sikar office, or get the same support remotely

How It Works

From Result Day to Admission Letter

What happens when a student comes to Campus Seva after JEE or NEET results.

1
Result & Rank Review
Our counselling team reviews the student's JEE/NEET rank and marks to understand realistic options.
2
JoSAA / Private Counselling
We handle every round of JoSAA and private counselling on the student's behalf — choice filling included.
3
Best-Fit College Match
We shortlist private & government colleges that genuinely match the student's rank, not just any seat.
4
Documentation by Us
Through our MoU with partner colleges, our team completes all admission paperwork and verification.
5
Settled — Hostel Included
We also help arrange hostel accommodation, so the student is fully settled before classes begin.
