Visa Guide

USA F1 Student Visa — Everything You Need to Know

F1 Visa Application Process

1

Receive I-20 from University

After admission, your US university sends the I-20 form — the official certificate of eligibility for F1 visa.

2

Pay SEVIS Fee (I-901)

Pay the $350 SEVIS fee online on the fmjfee.com portal. This is mandatory before applying for the F1 visa.

3

Fill DS-160 Application

Complete the online non-immigrant visa application (DS-160) on the US government portal. Campus Seva helps with this.

4

Schedule Visa Interview

Book your F1 visa interview at the nearest US Consulate (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad).

5

Attend Visa Interview

Appear for a 5–10 minute interview. Campus Seva's mock visa interview preparation gives you 98% success rate.

Common Questions

USA Study Abroad — FAQs

What is the eligibility to study in USA from India?+
For UG programs: 10+2 with 60%+ marks, SAT scores (optional at many universities), and English proficiency (IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL 80+). For PG programs: Bachelor's degree with 60%+ marks, GRE/GMAT scores (optional at many universities), and English proficiency. Campus Seva evaluates your profile for free and suggests the best-fit universities.
How much does it cost to study in USA for Indian students?+
Average tuition ranges from $15,000 to $55,000 per year depending on the university. Living expenses add another $10,000–$18,000/year. With Campus Seva's upto 50% scholarship, your total cost can be significantly reduced. Some universities also allow students to work part-time (20 hrs/week on campus) to cover living expenses.
Do I need GRE/GMAT to study in USA?+
Many US universities have made GRE/GMAT optional after COVID. However, submitting strong GRE/GMAT scores can improve your scholarship chances. For MBA programs, GMAT is still preferred at top business schools. Campus Seva counsellors guide you on whether you need these tests for your target universities.
What is the OPT visa and how long can I work after studying in USA?+
OPT (Optional Practical Training) allows F1 visa students to work in the USA after graduation — 1 year for most programs and 3 years for STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics) programs. This is one of the biggest advantages of studying in the USA compared to other countries.
How much scholarship can I get to study in USA?+
Through Campus Seva's partner universities, Indian students can get upto 50% merit-based scholarship on tuition fees. Additionally, university financial aid, departmental grants, Teaching Assistantships (TA) and Research Assistantships (RA) can cover even more. Campus Seva identifies all scholarships you qualify for at no charge.
Is Campus Seva's USA counselling really free?+
Yes, completely free! Campus Seva charges zero consultancy fee from students. We partner with universities who pay us a referral fee — so our service to you is 100% free. No registration, no application fee, no hidden charges. You only pay the university's application fee (if any) directly.
What is the best time to apply for USA universities?+
For Fall intake (Aug–Sep): Apply between October–March of the previous year. For Spring intake (Jan): Apply between June–October. We strongly recommend applying early — 12–18 months in advance — to maximize scholarship opportunities and university choices. Contact Campus Seva now to start your application process.
Can I get PR (Permanent Residency) in USA after studying?+
USA does not have a direct post-study PR route like Canada. However, after OPT work authorization, you can apply for H-1B work visa (employer-sponsored). Many Indian students eventually get US Green Card through their employer. The OPT period is crucial to establishing yourself in the US job market.
