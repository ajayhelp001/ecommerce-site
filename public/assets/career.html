Why We Started

Choosing a College Shouldn't Feel Like Gambling

Every year, lakhs of Indian students struggle to make one of the biggest decisions of their lives — which coaching institute to join, which college to apply to, where to live. Too often, that decision is made on rumors, agent commissions, or whoever shouts the loudest.

Campus Seva was built to fix that. We bring together verified information on coaching institutes, colleges, schools, hostels and study-abroad options — all in one place, completely free for students.

No paid rankings. No hidden agent fees. Just honest information, real reviews, and direct admission support — so every student can make the choice that's actually right for them.

Our Journey

How Campus Seva Came to Be

01

The Problem We Saw

Students in Kota and across Rajasthan were choosing coaching institutes based on hearsay and agent commissions — not real information.

02

Campus Seva Was Born

We built a platform to list verified coaching institutes and colleges with honest information — completely free for students to use.

03

Expanding Across India

From Kota and Jaipur, we grew to cover colleges, schools and hostels across India, building direct partnerships with leading institutions.

04

Going Global

We launched Study Abroad — connecting Indian students with 500+ universities across 8 countries, with scholarships up to 50%.

05

Today & Beyond

2,000+ students guided and counting. Our mission stays the same — honest education guidance, free for every student.
