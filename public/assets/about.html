Why we exist

The choice that shapes a career was being made on hearsay.

Every year, lakhs of students in Kota and across India pick a coaching institute or college without ever seeing honest information about it.

Fee structures hidden until enrolment day. Faculty claims nobody can verify. Hostel listings that don't match what's behind the gate. And running through almost every step, an agent whose advice depends on which institute pays the better commission.

"We didn't want to be another listings site. We wanted to be the place a student trusts more than the tenth person they ask."

So we built Campus Seva as the opposite of that system: verified institute information, real comparisons, and direct admission support — at zero cost to the student, because the student was never the one who should be paying.

How we operate

Three commitments we don't compromise on.

In the order we'd defend them, if asked.

01

The student never pays

Campus Seva is free for every student, on every plan, with no premium tier hiding better information behind a paywall. We're paid by partner institutes for successful, transparent referrals — never by ranking placement.

02

No listing is for sale

An institute's position on Campus Seva reflects verified facts — results, fees, faculty, infrastructure — not how much they've paid us. If we can't verify a claim, we don't publish it.

03

Every answer is specific

We don't give generic advice. A counsellor looks at your marks, budget, and goals before recommending anything — the same way a good mentor would, not a search algorithm.
